The Artist

Selling What
They Won't Say

Truth Seller is a voice built for the conversations people avoid. With a Bible open on the desk and the history books nobody finished on the shelf, every verse is a receipt — tracing where we came from, what it cost, and what we still owe each other.

The sound is raw and unpolished on purpose. Heavy drums, dusty samples, and lyrics written like testimony. This isn't music for the background. It's music that sits across the table and looks you in the eye.

"I don't sell comfort. I sell truth — and the price never goes down."

Released under Purpose Wave Entertainment, Truth Seller joins a roster of artists using new tools to tell old, unfinished stories.
